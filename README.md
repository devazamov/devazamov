## 📖 My Story

> *"Every expert was once a beginner who refused to give up."*

<table align="center">
<tr><td>

**How it started.** Like most developers of my generation, my journey began out of curiosity — wanting to know how the websites and apps I used every day were actually built. That curiosity turned into late nights with tutorials, broken code, and the addictive satisfaction of finally seeing something *run*.

**How it's going.** What started as tinkering has grown into a genuine full-stack discipline. I've moved from writing my first `<div>` to building complete systems: bots with real users, APIs backed by real databases, and interfaces designed to feel effortless. Along the way I picked up a second obsession — cybersecurity — because building software without understanding how it can be attacked felt incomplete.

**Where I am now.** I build in public, ship real projects (including AI-powered Telegram bots serving real communities), and treat every project as a chance to raise my own bar — cleaner code, better architecture, tighter security, sharper design.

**Where I'm going.** My goal is to keep compounding: deeper backend architecture, more advanced AI integration, serious security skills, and eventually, products and open-source tools used by developers far beyond Uzbekistan.

</td></tr>
</table>
